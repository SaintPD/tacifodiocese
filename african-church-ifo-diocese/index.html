Our Heritage

A Historic Church Rooted in African Soil

The African Church was established on 13th October 1901 as an indigenous church, holding fast to orthodox Christian faith with the Holy Bible as the ultimate guide. The Ifo Diocese was formally inaugurated on 7th April 2018.

The Crest of The African Church

Bearing the open Holy Bible in heavenly radiance with our divine motto "Heaven's Light Our Guide", commemorating our foundation date: Established 13th Oct. 1901.
